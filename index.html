    Show it on your TV

    1. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi.
    2. Choose your videos and tap Play full screen.
    3. Open your phone’s quick settings, tap Cast or Smart View, and pick your TV. The TV mirrors the loop.

    If your browser supports it, the Cast to TV button inside the player sends the current clip straight to the TV. Keep the phone plugged in while it mirrors. If the TV has its own web browser, you can also open this page there and pick videos on the TV.

    Videos are read on your device and never uploaded.
